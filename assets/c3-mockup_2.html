How this is calculated

What ARKI knows about the building

ARKI uses every flat's dimensions, occupancy, twelve months of usage history, this week's forecast, regional grid load, and the building's contract with the grid operator. For your flat (4C, 62 m², 2 + dog): your share is calculated as 78 kWh this week.

How ARKI sets the building plan

The 1,150 kWh weekly building pool comes from the grid contract for heatwave weeks. ARKI distributes it across the 14 flats based on size and occupancy, then sets per-day setpoints to keep the building under regional brownout thresholds. When forecasts shift mid-week — like Friday's record forecast — ARKI proposes adjustments and asks the building to decide.

What ARKI does in this version of the app

ARKI sets the plan; the building decides on changes. You can accept, decline, or propose an alternative to ARKI's plan, but you cannot freely change setpoints without building review. Decline notes are visible to other flats. Approved requests appear in the building log. ARKI does not override the building's collective decision.

The numbers

All estimates. Actual use varies with humidity, window shading, and how often the cooling system cycles.
