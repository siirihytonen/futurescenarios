How this is calculated

What ARKI knows about you

ARKI uses your flat's dimensions (62 m², west-facing, 4th floor), the cooling system's measured efficiency, the seven-day local forecast, and twelve months of your own usage history. Last week you used 38 kWh. Last summer's average was 44 kWh/week.

How the building pool is set

The 1,400 kWh weekly pool comes from your building's grid contract for heatwave weeks. ARKI distributes a starting allocation to each flat based on size and occupancy, then opens the pool for the week — what you don't plan, others can draw on, and vice versa.

What ARKI does in this version of the app

ARKI advises but does not decide. It surfaces requests from neighbors, calculates what the building pool can absorb, and shows you the consequences of your choices in real time. It does not set your setpoints, auto-adjust them, or override what you plan. The plan you save is the plan the building uses.

The numbers

One "cool day for the building" ≈ 168 kWh (fourteen flats running moderate cooling for one day). Appliance comparisons use 2024–2025 reference values:

  • Refrigerator, full day — 1.5 kWh (ENERGY STAR modern full-size)
  • Laundry, cold cycle — 0.7 kWh per load
  • Dinner on stovetop, 1 hour — 1.0 kWh
  • Laptop, 8-hour workday — 0.4 kWh
  • LED lights, 4-hour evening — 0.2 kWh

All estimates. Actual use varies with humidity, window shading, door opening, and how often the cooling system cycles.
