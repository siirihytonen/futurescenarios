How this is calculated

What ARKI knows about you

ARKI uses your flat's dimensions (62 m², west-facing, 4th floor), the cooling system's measured efficiency, the seven-day forecast, twelve months of your own usage history, and your registered work pattern for this week. Last week you used 38 kWh. Last summer's average was 37 kWh/week.

How your plan was generated

Sunday morning, ARKI received this week's forecast and your registered early-shift schedule. It generated a setpoint plan that fits within your 78 kWh weekly budget. The budget is personal to your household, based on your flat's size and occupancy. This version of the app does not show building-level totals.

What ARKI does in this version of the app

ARKI generates and applies your plan. It will adjust setpoints during the week if forecasts change — as it did Sunday afternoon for Thursday — to keep your week within budget. You can request overrides, but ARKI reviews them on its own schedule. ARKI does not communicate with other flats or share your data with the building.

The numbers
