A blackout in 2031 left some districts without power for days while some were untouched. Since then each home in a building gets the same allocation. When a heat alert opens, your building's allocation is worked out from the size of the building, the weather forecast, the building's sensor readings and the residents' calendars, then split equally between the flats. Your share doesn't grow when you need more. You can apply for an exception, but applications close 24 hours before the alert. ARKI divides the allocation and puts it into effect.
